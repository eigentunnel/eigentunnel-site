About

Eigen Systems builds EigenTunnel.

We're a small team building a hybrid post-quantum overlay that's honest about where it is: public alpha, real code, not yet independently audited.

Why the name changed

Previously FreeQ. Now EigenTunnel.

This product was previously marketed as FreeQ. That name collided with an unrelated influencer's site, so everything — the product name, the repos, the domain — was renamed to EigenTunnel. The overlay itself, the cryptography, and the roadmap did not change. If you have old links or docs referencing FreeQ or getfreeq.com, they now point to EigenTunnel and eigentunnel.com.

Why now

Harvest-now-decrypt-later is not hypothetical.

Traffic encrypted with classical-only key exchange today can be recorded now and decrypted later, once large-scale quantum computers exist. For data with a multi-year confidentiality requirement — infrastructure telemetry, credentials, long-lived secrets — that clock is already running. EigenTunnel exists so teams can start hybrid PQC migration on real traffic paths now, without waiting for a mandate.

Integration-first

Designed to sit alongside what you already run, not replace it. Overlay, not rip-and-replace.

Evidence, not vibes

CBOM discovery gives you an inventory of what's actually protected, so audits and migrations are based on data.

Free core, by design

The cryptographic protection layer is AGPL v3 and stays free. Cloud monetizes fleet operations, not the crypto.
