The problem

Post-quantum security was not built for bad links.

Post-quantum encryption is coming to every network that carries sensitive data. The new algorithms are stronger, but they need much larger setup messages than today's encryption. On a degraded radio net, a satellite link or a low-power cellular modem, those larger setups often stall or fail. When the secure link cannot connect, operators lose the connection or fall back to weaker protection.

What EigenTunnel delivers

Connects where ordinary VPNs give up.

Designed to set up and renew quantum-resistant sessions over lossy, narrowband and intermittent links.

Stays secure through the drops.

Built to keep keys fresh through dropouts, coverage gaps and planned outages, with no downgrade in protection.

Light on the link.

Low overhead compared with plain traffic, so small pipes stay useful.
