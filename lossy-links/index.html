The problem

Post-quantum security was not built for bad links.

Post-quantum encryption is coming to every network that carries sensitive data. The new algorithms are stronger, but their setup messages are much larger than today's. On a degraded radio net, a satellite link or a low-power cellular modem, those larger setups often stall or fail. When the secure link cannot connect, operators lose the connection or fall back to weaker protection.

EigenTunnel is built to close that gap.

What EigenTunnel delivers
