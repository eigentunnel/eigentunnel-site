Questions

Pricing FAQ

Will Core ever stop being free?

No. Core's interop profile ships under AGPL v3 and stays free forever for lab, evaluation, and interop testing. Production deployments that need the locked CNSA 2.0 profile run under Cloud.

How is Cloud priced?

By enrolled node count and which pieces you need. The CNSA 2.0 production profile is ready now; CBOM/compliance evidence is prototype, and fleet enrollment, rendezvous, and relay are roadmap we shape with design partners. We scope this on a call rather than guessing at a self-serve tier that doesn't fit your fleet.

Can we pilot before committing?

Yes — that's the default path right now. Most engagements start as a scoped pilot against a real segment of your network.
