Before you commit

What is free, and what requires approval?

The public Python tools are available on GitHub. Standard free binary lab access requires manual registration review, with no payment or NDA required. Use a work, government or academic email; independent consultants can request individual review. Core source remains private and requires separately approved access terms.

Who hosts the gateway?

For the free lab, you host and pay for every node, including any gateway in your own private cloud or cloud account. Eigen-Systems-hosted infrastructure requires a paid agreement before resources are provisioned. Self-hosting a gateway does not require buying a managed Cloud service.

How much does a paid engagement cost?

We quote the defined scope. Connection count, environments, integration work, evidence requirements and support needs inform the quote. The written offer identifies fees, deliverables and any recurring charges; there is no self-service checkout or published standard subscription price today.

What does the license cover?

The agreement specifies the permitted capabilities, environments, endpoints or sites, term, update rights and support. Redistribution and OEM embedding require explicit rights. A lab evaluation does not grant production or redistribution rights.

What happens when an evaluation or agreement ends?

Continued binary use, renewal, updates, support and any hosted-service shutdown are addressed in the written terms before purchase. Indefinite use of paid binaries is not currently a published entitlement. The public Python tools remain subject to their published open-source license.

Does payment buy certification or authorization?

No. Payment buys the rights, work and deliverables in the agreement. Developer tests, a pilot result or draft compliance artifacts do not confer independent assurance or an agency authorization. Review the evidence and limitations before selecting a scope.
