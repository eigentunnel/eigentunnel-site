Use case · Cellular IoT

Quantum-ready security for Cat-M field devices.

Cat-M and LTE-M devices live at the edge of coverage on small data plans, often for ten years or more. EigenTunnel gives their links quantum-resistant protection that connects on weak signal and stays light on the wire.

What you get

Connects on weak signal

Built for the marginal coverage where field devices actually sit: basements, vaults, rural poles and remote pads.

Light on data plans

Designed to keep protection overhead low, so security does not blow up per-device data costs.

Works with long-lived devices

Protect the path through a gateway or endpoint without waiting for a full device refresh.

Ready for quantum

Field devices outlive encryption choices. Quantum-resistant protection now avoids a second retrofit later.

Where it fits

  • Utility meters, sensors and reclosers
  • Water and wastewater remote monitoring
  • Pipeline and tank telemetry
  • Remote OT gateways for critical infrastructure
