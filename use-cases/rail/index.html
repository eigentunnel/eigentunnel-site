Use case · Rail

Secure rail links that keep up with the train.

Rail communications run over narrowband radio, cellular and long wayside runs where coverage comes and goes. EigenTunnel protects those links with quantum-resistant encryption and keeps them connected on the network you already run.

What you get

Built for narrowband radio

Designed for low-bandwidth, high-loss links where ordinary VPNs struggle to connect at all.

Stays up through coverage gaps

Protection continues as trains and crews move between cells, tunnels and remote territory.

Keeps existing equipment in place

Protection at the connection layer, so long-lived wayside and onboard equipment does not need to be replaced first.

Ready for quantum

Rail assets run for decades. Quantum-resistant protection keeps today's recorded traffic from becoming tomorrow's exposure.

Where it fits

  • Wayside to back-office data links
  • Onboard to office telemetry and maintenance data
  • Yard and remote-site connectivity
  • Railroad security plan modernization

Why now

FRA's PTC communications cybersecurity study reviews cryptographic options for constrained rail endpoints and identifies post-quantum options. FRA. Rail security requirements are set by each railroad's plan and FRA rules. EigenTunnel is evaluated as part of that process.

How to start

  1. Book a 20-minute call about your link.
  2. Agree on one connection and what success looks like.
  3. Run a time-boxed pilot on your own link and decide with the results in hand.
