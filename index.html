Why it matters

Protect traffic with a long shelf life.

Harvest-now-decrypt-later is the primary threat model: traffic captured today can be decrypted once large-scale quantum computers arrive. Anything with a multi-year confidentiality requirement is exposed now, not later.

Hybrid tunnels

X25519 + ML-KEM-768 (FIPS 203). AES-256-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305 bulk.

Mutual auth

ML-DSA-65 (FIPS 204). No session unless the peer proves a registered identity.

Agility

Parameter sets can move with standards. Forward secrecy per session.

Connectivity

Direct: Node A -> EigenTunnel overlay -> Node B
Relay:  Node A --outbound only--> gateway <--outbound only-- Node B
