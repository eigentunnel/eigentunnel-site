The problem

Your systems may need to stay. Their connections need a migration plan.

Long-lived equipment

Replacing an entire device or application may be difficult. Evaluate whether protection at the connection layer fits your constraints.

Difficult network access

Remote sites can sit behind network address translation (NAT), which makes direct connections difficult. A relay provides an intermediate connection point.

An investment decision

Before funding a wider migration, establish what works, what needs integration, and which risks remain.

What has worked

Two Macs. An AWS relay. A remote endpoint behind Starlink.

EigenTunnel’s founder reports a successful macOS → AWS → macOS relay/gateway test with the far endpoint behind Starlink NAT. This demonstrates connectivity in that tested setup. The commercial workflows for Cloud payment and provisioning are unfinished.

The connectivity test does not establish which cryptographic profile was used. The private CNSA 2.0 algorithm profile has separate developer-test evidence; independent assessment and production deployment evidence are pending.
