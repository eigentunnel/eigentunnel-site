The problem

Your systems may need to stay. Their connections need a migration plan.

Long-lived equipment

Replacing an entire device or application may be difficult. Evaluate whether protection at the connection layer fits your constraints.

Difficult network access

Remote sites can sit behind network address translation (NAT), which makes direct connections difficult. A relay provides an intermediate connection point.

An investment decision

What has worked

Two Mac®s. An AWS relay. A remote endpoint behind Starlink.

EigenTunnel’s founder reports a successful macOS® → AWS (Amazon Web Services) → macOS relay/gateway test with the far endpoint behind a Starlink satellite internet NAT. This demonstrates connectivity in that tested setup. The commercial workflows for the Console’s payment and provisioning are unfinished.

The connectivity test does not establish which cryptographic profile was used. The private CNSA 2.0 algorithm profile (CNSA is the NSA’s Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite) has separate developer-test evidence; independent assessment and production deployment evidence are pending.

Measured on November 10, 2026

A live Cat-M road test, checked at the receiver.

A rail-style rig (an STM32-class board, a Cat-M modem, and a Dell relay) ran a live road test over a commercial LTE Cat-M link. The route went from Omaha to Ashland and Greenwood, Nebraska, and back: 66.5 miles, alongside BNSF along US-6.

19 of 19

Drive transfers delivered and verified at the AWS (Amazon Web Services) receiver, plus 20 more after lunch.

3 for 3

Unplug/replug passes self-recovered to verified delivery with no human help.

122 bytes

Session resume, measured. A full handshake is about 8 KB.

Session security was a fresh authenticated post-quantum handshake: ML-KEM-1024 key exchange and ML-DSA-87 signatures, with no pre-shared keys, so there is no central key database to steal. The tests ran on Red Hat® Enterprise Linux® 9.8 with the operating system in FIPS mode.

These figures are the measured results of that test. They do not establish a rail deployment, a safety approval, or a service-level commitment.
