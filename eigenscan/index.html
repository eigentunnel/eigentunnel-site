Carry-in network appliance

EigenScan

EigenScan is a carry-in network appliance. It discovers cryptography on a bounded network segment and produces a CycloneDX 1.6 cryptographic bill of materials (CBOM), plus a daily delta.

Lab-proven connectors cover Splunk (HEC), Elastic, Microsoft Sentinel, ServiceNow, Jira, and n8n.
