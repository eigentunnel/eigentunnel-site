## The short version

EigenTunnel gives you quantum-resistant secure links that connect and stay up on the networks missions actually use: degraded tactical radio, satellite, Cat-M cellular and rail radio. The approach is patent pending (U.S. Application No. 64/175,794). We share technical detail and performance data with evaluation partners under a mutual NDA.

## Why this matters now

Post-quantum encryption is coming to every network that carries sensitive data. The new algorithms are stronger, but their setup messages are much larger than today's. On a clean office network that hardly matters. On a degraded radio net, a satellite pass or a low-power cellular modem, those larger setups often stall or fail outright. When the secure link cannot connect, operators either lose the connection or fall back to weaker protection. Neither is acceptable.

## Outcomes you can expect to evaluate

- **Connects where ordinary VPN handshakes fail.** EigenTunnel is designed to set up and renew quantum-resistant sessions over lossy, narrowband and intermittent links.
- **Stays secure through disruption.** Sessions are built to keep fresh keys through dropouts, coverage gaps and planned outages, without downgrading protection.
- **Light on the link.** Protected traffic adds little cost compared with sending the same traffic in the clear, which matters when every kilobit counts.
- **Ready for the post-quantum transition.** Designed for standards-based post-quantum cryptography, alone or paired with today's classical algorithms.

## Use cases

**Sensor fusion and C2.** Edge sensors and command-and-control nodes rarely get a clean pipe. EigenTunnel is built to carry secure backhaul from sensors to fusion and C2 systems over whatever link is available, so the data path stays protected when the network is at its worst. Integrators can embed it in sensor, edge and C2 platforms rather than bolting on a separate box.

**Government post-quantum transition.** Agencies and programs are planning their move to quantum-resistant encryption, and deployed and remote users are the hardest part of that plan. EigenTunnel is designed to bring quantum-resistant protection to the far edge of the network, where the larger post-quantum setups are most likely to fail on conventional tools. It supports pairing post-quantum with classical algorithms during the transition.

**Rail and critical infrastructure.** Rail operations depend on narrowband radio and long, remote corridors with uneven coverage. EigenTunnel is built to keep wayside, onboard and back-office links secure through coverage gaps, without asking operators to replace the radios they already run.
