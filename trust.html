Technical diligence package

Core source remains private. Request scope and availability of architecture and implementation material under separately approved access terms; an NDA alone does not grant access. A complete reviewed assurance package is not yet published.

  • Protocol/state machine, hybrid combiner, transcript binding and downgrade protection: consolidated reviewer package pending.

  • Security reports: contact Patrick privately to arrange reporting. Do not send secrets or exploit details through the general inquiry form. No response-time SLA claimed.
