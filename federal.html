Claims discipline

What this page should say clearly

EigenTunnel is not currently presenting itself as FedRAMP authorized, FIPS 140-3 validated, ATLAS authorized, approved for classified systems, or suitable for life-safety operation. Those are roadmap/partner/governance paths.

  2. Run cryptographic discovery against the approved scope.
  3. Map findings into CBOM and OSCAL-oriented evidence where appropriate.
  4. Select one protected-path pilot candidate.
  5. Document limits, unknowns, and next authorization work.
