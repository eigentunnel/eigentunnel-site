Encryption status

Current public cryptography story

Public lab kernel

Core Public exposes hybrid X25519 plus ML-KEM key establishment, ML-DSA identity signatures, AEAD packet protection, session derivation, and validation tests.

Claims boundary

The public branch is alpha lab code and not independently audited. It is not a FIPS 140-3 validation, FedRAMP authorization, or approval for classified or life-safety systems.

Private advancement

Private Core carries a locked CNSA 2.0 profile — ML-KEM-1024 + ML-DSA-87 (Category 5) + AES-256-GCM, fail-closed at process start and peer admission. It's implemented with developer-test evidence; production evidence and independent assessment are still pending. Other product hardening, performance work, and packaging remain private until released under explicit terms.

Evidence and CBOM

Roadmap/partner work

OSCAL and federal artifacts

OSCAL-oriented mappings (targeting OSCAL v1.2.2, the version named in current RFI evidence requests), control narratives, POA&M inputs, and federal evidence packages are support work. They have no authorization effect.

Connectivity

Direct when possible. Managed relay when justified.

Relay/gateway connectivity tested by the founder over macOS → AWS → macOS, with the far endpoint behind Starlink NAT. Cloud payment and provisioning workflows are unfinished. This is a reported end-to-end connectivity test, not evidence of general availability or an independently assessed managed service. The test does not establish which cryptographic profile was used.

Claim boundary: EigenTunnel protects traffic routed through EigenTunnel-controlled paths. It does not patch the original endpoint, protect unmanaged bypass traffic, or replace human approval for OT and regulated environments.
