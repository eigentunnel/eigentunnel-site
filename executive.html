When a conversation makes sense

You operate long-lived systems

You need to evaluate connection-level protection while understanding endpoint, safety and integration constraints.

You build equipment or services

You want to investigate adding this capability to a product and need to establish engineering effort, licensing and support requirements.

Why pay for an evaluation?

The proposed value is a narrower, better-informed migration decision. A scoped engagement can define the connection to test, the agreed measurements, integration requirements and unresolved limitations. It should end with evidence you can use to proceed, revise the approach or stop.

Savings, deployment speed and operational impact must be measured in your environment. They are not established product guarantees.

What to agree before signing

  1. The business problem and one named connection.
  2. Approved systems, test environment, exclusions and customer responsibilities.
  3. Deliverables, measurements and acceptance criteria.
  4. Price, schedule, support and data-handling terms.
  5. The decision owner and the criteria for further investment.

What is ready—and what is pending

Founder-tested relay connectivity: macOS → AWS → macOS, including a remote endpoint behind Starlink NAT. Separately, the private CNSA 2.0 algorithm profile has developer-test evidence. Cloud payment and provisioning remain unfinished; fleet workflows remain roadmap. Independent assessment and production evidence are pending.

A pilot is not a purchase of an independently validated, generally available managed service. Formal authorization is not conferred by the available evidence drafts.
