Proposed evaluation scenarios

Practical questions for your technical team.

These are proposed lab uses, not customer deployments or production approvals.

Remote utilities and facilities

Represent a remote monitoring site with synthetic traffic. Test a connection through your gateway, including reconnect behavior and whether traffic can bypass the intended path.

Campus and research networks

Connect two lab machines or an off-site research endpoint. Record compatibility, delivery and operational limits before considering a broader migration.

Agency-controlled gateways

Evaluate a node → your gateway → node topology across a difficult network boundary. Establish which routes and endpoints your team must operate.

Why evaluate

Documented risks. A bounded response.

CISA has documented attacks against internet-exposed controllers, including water-sector systems. A protected connection is useful only when the surrounding deployment also restricts unwanted access and bypass routes. EigenTunnel does not patch controllers or replace authentication, backups or monitoring. Read the CISA advisory.

New York’s wastewater rules provide one state-specific example: requirements address access control, authentication and network structure, with relevant controls taking effect March 11, 2027. This is context for engineering decisions, not an EigenTunnel compliance claim. NYS DEC requirements.

University researchers have documented PQC migration work on scientific networks. A reproducible lab exercise can help teams ask similar questions about their own connections. PQC network instrument research.

Quantum readiness is a planning question.

Executive Order 14412 of June 22, 2026 directs federal PQC transition work and assistance to critical-infrastructure operators. Federal deadlines are not blanket requirements for every state agency, city or school. Read the executive order.

For schools, foundational measures such as MFA, patching and tested backups remain priorities. A connection evaluation should address a specific need alongside those measures. Education Department guidance.

Policy context reviewed September 6, 2026. Applicability depends on the organization and deployment.
